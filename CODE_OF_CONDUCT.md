# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Critique behavior and artifacts rather than people.
Preserve contributor privacy, credit source work, and make safety or ownership uncertainty explicit.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential
sharing, fabricated validation claims, and pressure to bypass approval gates are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner may remove contributions or
restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.
